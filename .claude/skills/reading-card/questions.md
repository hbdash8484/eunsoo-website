# Reading & Reflections — Question Sheet

The printed question sheet Danielle uses with her reading card (header: "Danielle's reading card · kimeunsoo.xyz").
This file is the master copy. It is not shown on the website.

---

**Pick 3–5 questions.** Start with a question that looks fun, choose from at least two groups, and try one ◐ **stretch**.
Write your answers on the reading card — put each question's number in its **Q** box.

● start here  ◐ a good stretch

## 📖 Look closely

1. ● **The whoa moment** — Which part made you stop and go "whoa" — a scene, a line, or an idea? What was it about that part that grabbed you?
2. ◐ **Word detective** — Pick ONE sentence you loved. Why do you think the author wrote it *that* way — the words, the sound, the picture it makes in your head?
   *Try starting: The author wrote "___." I think they chose those words because…*
3. ◐ **Start & finish** — Why do you think the book *starts* where it starts and *ends* where it ends? What would change if it began or stopped somewhere else?
4. ◐ **The missing voice** — Whose side of the story do we *not* get to hear? How might the book feel different if that person got to tell it?
   *Try starting: We never hear from ___. If they told it, we'd find out…*

## 🔍 Think it through

5. ● **In their shoes** — Why did the character do what they did? What were they thinking or feeling that made it make sense to *them* — even if you'd have done it differently?
6. ● **The other side** — Which character did you disagree with the most? Can you explain why *they* thought they were right?
7. ◐ **Real life** — What problem in the book reminds you of something real in the world today? Is it exactly the same, or does the book make you see it in a new way?
8. ◐ **The hidden rule** — Every world has rules nobody says out loud. What's one hidden rule in this book's world, and who does it help or hurt?
   *Try starting: Everyone acts like ___ even though nobody ever says it.*
9. ◐ **The big idea** — If the whole book had one secret message, what would it be in a few words? Then — what does that message leave out?
   *Try starting: This book is really about ___. But it doesn't say much about…*

## 💬 Make it yours

10. ● **Changed my mind** — Did the book make you think differently about something? What did you believe before, and what do you think now?
11. ● **Still wondering** — What's one big question the book never answered for you? Do you think the author left it open on purpose?
12. ● **Project spark** — Could an idea from this book show up in one of your own drawings, games, or projects? What would you borrow — and how would you make it *yours*?
13. ● **Book buddies** — What other book, movie, or show does this remind you of? Do they agree with each other, or would they argue?

---

*Reading a true story or a memoir? Read "character" as the real person, and "story" as what the writer is trying to tell you.*
